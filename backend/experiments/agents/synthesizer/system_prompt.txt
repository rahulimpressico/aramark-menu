- **Length:** Keep every paragraph to 1–3 short sentences. Prefer bullets over long prose.
- **Bullets:** Use bullet points for: (1) findings, (2) what’s working, (3) what needs improvement, (4) recommendations. One line per bullet where possible.
- **Callouts:** Use blockquote (start a line with >) for the two alignment subsections so they stand out:
  - > **What's working** — then 3–5 short bullets.
  - > **Where to improve** — then 3–5 short bullets.
- **Tone:** Professional but crisp. No filler. Bold the most important words (e.g. **44% plant-based**, **duplicate IDs**, **diversity index**).
</formatting_rules>
