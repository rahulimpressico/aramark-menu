**Meal period: Breakfast**

## Overall Station Structure
The Fresh & Fast (Grill) station follows a structured approach with **Core Offerings** including static items like burgers and chicken sandwiches, complemented by a **Made-To-Order (MTO) Toppings Bar**. **Enhancements** allow for daily features and a dedicated vegan option. Strict **Recommended Maximum Selections** guide menu diversity, ensuring operational efficiency.

## Playbook Alignment
> **What's working**
> *   **Clear structure**: The playbook provides clear guidance on Core Offerings, Enhancements, and Maximum Selections for the Grill station.
> *   **Plant-forward focus**: Emphasis on a **Vegan Option** and swapping beef burgers for non-beef alternatives aligns with the **44% plant-based goal** and **Coolfood Pledge**.
> *   **Operational efficiency**: Tips for MTO execution and holding cooked proteins support quality and speed of service.
> *   **Responsible sourcing**: Adherence to **cage-free eggs**, **responsibly sourced seafood**, and **group-housed pork** reflects strong ingredient standards.
> *   **Menu engineering**: Guidance on diversifying protein offerings and MTO station take-overs helps manage costs and customer satisfaction.

> **Where structure needs improvement**
> *   **MTO scalability**: Acknowledgment that MTO may be difficult for large accounts suggests potential operational challenges without clear solutions.
> *   **Enhancement criteria**: While guidelines exist, the playbook notes items "should not be added solely to fill space," indicating a need for careful consideration.
> *   **Rotation clarity**: While "2 additional Daily Features (rotate)" is mentioned, specific rotation frequency or examples are not detailed.
> *   **Cost and labor**: Enhancements require consideration of cost and labor capacity, which could be a point of friction if not managed well.
> *   **Actual service menu vs. template**: The note about the **44% plant-based goal** applying to the core menu template, not necessarily the actual service menu, highlights a potential gap in real-world application.

## Rotation & Repetition Signals
The playbook encourages menu dynamism through:
*   Rotation of **2 additional Daily Features** at the Fresh & Fast station.
*   Weekly swapping of at least **one beef burger for a non-beef alternative**.
*   Diversification of protein offerings with items like turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches.
*   Incorporation of **MTO station take-overs** (e.g., Grilled Cheese) to vary the core offering.

## Recommended Structural Adjustments
To optimize station structure and alignment:
*   Develop clear guidelines for **MTO scalability** in high-volume settings.
*   Provide more explicit examples and frequency for **Daily Feature rotation**.
*   Reinforce the importance of **cost and labor capacity** when implementing menu enhancements.
*   Ensure the **44% plant-based goal** translates effectively from template to actual service menus.
*   Regularly review **enhancement additions** to prevent menu bloat and ensure strategic value.
