## What the Playbook Recommends

1.  Reduce the number of daily features to a maximum of 2 per day. — Playbook rule: "DAILY FEATURE: Maximum 2 per day."
2.  Add at least one plant-based main item to the menu each day. — Playbook rule: "VEGAN: Minimum 1 plant-based main item per day."
3.  Include at least one fried starch item on the menu each day. — Playbook rule: "FRIES: Minimum 1 fried starch per day."
4.  Improve the rotation of entrees, especially those appearing on 4 or more days. — Playbook rule: "ROTATION: High repetition of any entree across multiple days signals poor rotation."
5.  Increase the percentage of plant-based entrees to meet the 44% target. — Playbook rule: "PLANT-BASED: 44% of entrees should be plant-based (Aramark Coolfood Pledge)."
