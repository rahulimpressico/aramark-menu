## Overall Station Structure
- This report covers the **Breakfast** meal period for the Grill station.
- The current menu offers a wide variety of items, including 60 daily features and 20 vegan options, but lacks core items like burgers and french fries.

## Playbook Alignment with **Where to improve:**
> - **Burger** — Add one **Burger** to align with core offerings and recommended maximum selections.
> - **Daily Features** — Significantly reduce **Daily Features** from 60 to the recommended maximum of 2 to ensure efficient execution and quality standards.
> - **Vegan Option** — Reduce **Vegan Option** offerings from 20 to the recommended maximum of 1 to optimize menu structure.
> - **French Fries** — Add **French Fries** to the menu as a core offering.

## Rotation & Repetition
- The menu demonstrates good variety with a diversity index of 1.0 and 16 unique entrees, indicating a low monotony risk.
- No high-repetition signals were identified for individual recipes, suggesting a well-rotated menu.

## Recommended Adjustments
- Introduce one **Burger** and one **French Fry** offering to meet the core menu requirements for the Grill station.
- Streamline the menu by reducing **Daily Features** to a maximum of two rotating options and the **Vegan Option** to a single offering.
- Review the current extensive offerings to ensure they do not exceed labor capacity or compromise quality, as indicated by the high counts for daily features and vegan options.
