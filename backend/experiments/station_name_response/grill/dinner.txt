**Meal period: Dinner**

## Overall Station Structure
The Fresh & Fast (Grill) station features a **Core Offerings** structure including a static menu of burgers, chicken sandwiches, hot dogs, and fries, complemented by a **Made-To-Order (MTO) Toppings Bar**. **Enhancements** include a rotating Daily Feature, a dedicated Vegan Option, and French Fries. The playbook recommends a maximum of **1 Burger**, **2 additional Daily Features**, a **Vegan Option**, and a **French Fry**.

## Playbook Alignment
> **What's working**
> *   **Ingredient Standards**: Compliance with **cage-free eggs**, **responsibly sourced seafood**, and **group-housed pork**.
> *   **Plant-Based Focus**: Strong commitment to **plant-based meal options** and the **44% plant-forward goal** by 2025.
> *   **Climate Commitments**: Adherence to **Net-Zero** targets and the **Coolfood Pledge**.
> *   **Menu Engineering**: Strategic use of non-beef alternatives to lower **CPM by 18%** while maintaining satisfaction.
> *   **Service Method**: Clear guidelines for **cooked-to-order** and proper display for self-serve.

> **Where to improve**
> *   **Holding Practices**: Ensure strict adherence to the guideline: **do not hold cooked proteins in water or broth baths**.
> *   **Menu Selection Limits**: Avoid exceeding **recommended maximum selections** to maintain operational efficiency and quality.
> *   **MTO Execution**: For large accounts, simplify **Made-to-Order (MTO)** menus with condiment bars to support speed of service.
> *   **Item Justification**: Ensure **enhancements** are added based on demand and not solely to fill space, considering cost and labor.

## Rotation & Repetition Signals
The playbook emphasizes rotation to maintain menu diversity and guest satisfaction. Key signals include:
*   **Daily Features**: Rotate **2 additional Daily Features** at the Fresh & Fast station.
*   **Protein Diversification**: Swap out at least **one beef burger for a non-beef alternative each week**.
*   **Variety**: Diversify protein offerings with options like turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches.
*   **MTO Take-overs**: Incorporate MTO station take-overs, such as Grilled Cheese, to vary offerings.

## Recommended Structural Adjustments
To optimize station performance and playbook alignment:
*   **Adhere to Maximum Selections**: Strictly follow the recommended maximums for menu items to prevent operational strain.
*   **Implement Protein Rotation**: Systematically rotate daily features and non-beef alternatives weekly as per guidelines.
*   **Review Holding Protocols**: Reinforce proper holding techniques for cooked proteins to ensure quality and safety.
*   **Streamline MTO for Volume**: For high-volume periods, simplify MTO offerings to enhance speed of service and guest experience.
