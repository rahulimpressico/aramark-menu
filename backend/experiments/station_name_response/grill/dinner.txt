## Overall Station Structure
- The **Dinner** meal period at the Grill station offers a core menu of Made-To-Order (MTO) items including burgers, chicken sandwiches, hot dogs, and fries, complemented by an MTO Toppings Bar. Additional proteins are available based on demand.
- Current weekly unique offerings include 15 **burgers**, 35 **daily features**, 20 **vegan options**, and 5 **fries**.

## Playbook Alignment with **Where to improve:**
> - **Burger**: Limit daily offerings to one distinct burger type to align with recommended maximum selections, addressing the current over-offering on multiple days.
> - **Daily Feature**: Reduce the number of daily features offered to a maximum of two rotating options per day, as current offerings exceed this guideline.
> - **Vegan Option**: Streamline daily vegan offerings to one distinct option to improve operational efficiency and align with playbook guidelines, given the high number of unique options currently offered.

## Rotation & Repetition
- The menu demonstrates good variety with a diversity index of 1.0 and 15 unique entree counts, indicating a low risk of monotony for guests.
- No high-repetition signals were identified, suggesting a well-rotated menu across the week.

## Recommended Adjustments
- **Optimize Offerings**: Reduce the daily selection of **burgers**, **daily features**, and **vegan options** to meet the playbook's recommended maximums (1 burger, 2 daily features, 1 vegan option per day) to enhance execution efficiency and quality.
- **Diversify Proteins**: Implement the chef tip to swap at least one **beef burger** for a non-beef alternative (e.g., turkey, chicken, or plant-based) each week to lower Cost Per Meal (CPM) and support sustainability goals.
- **Menu Simplification**: For high-volume periods, plan a simplified menu with a robust condiment/toppings bar to maintain speed of service while adhering to offering limits.
