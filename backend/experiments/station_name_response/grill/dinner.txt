**Meal period: Dinner**

## Overall Station Structure
The station structure emphasizes **Core Offerings** with flexible **Enhancements** to meet specific needs. Clear **Recommended Maximum Selections** are provided to ensure efficient execution and quality standards. The Fresh & Fast (Grill) station, for example, includes a static core with Made-To-Order (MTO) ingredients and a toppings bar, complemented by rotating daily features and vegan options.

## Playbook Alignment
> **What's working:**
> *   **Cage-free eggs**, **responsibly sourced seafood**, and **group-housed pork** demonstrate strong ethical sourcing.
> *   Commitment to **plant-based meal options** supports diverse diets and sustainability goals.
> *   Emphasis on **fresh, seasonal produce** and **regional/global menu diversity** enhances guest experience.
> *   Integration of "Be Well. Do Well." highlights provides valuable health and sustainability tips.
> *   Clear guidelines for **Core Offerings** and **Enhancements** provide a structured approach to menu building.

> **Where to improve:**
> *   Ensure strict adherence to **Recommended Maximum Selections** to prevent operational inefficiencies and maintain quality.
> *   Monitor compliance with **44% plant-based** menu goal, ensuring core menu templates reflect this target.
> *   Reinforce proper protein **holding techniques** to avoid water/broth baths, as per Chef Tips.
> *   Optimize MTO station execution for large accounts, potentially through simplified menus and robust condiment bars.
> *   Regularly review menu offerings for consistency and adherence to playbook guidelines.

## Rotation & Repetition Signals
Effective menu rotation is crucial for guest satisfaction and operational efficiency.
*   Implement the recommended **2 additional Daily Features (rotate)** at stations like Fresh & Fast.
*   Actively **swap out at least one beef burger for a non-beef alternative each week** to diversify protein offerings and lower CPM.
*   Incorporate creative MTO station take-overs, such as **Grilled Cheese**, to provide variety.
*   Ensure consistent diversification of protein offerings, including turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches.

## Recommended Adjustments
To optimize menu structure and operational flow:
*   Strictly enforce **Recommended Maximum Selections** across all stations.
*   Prioritize **protein diversification and weekly rotation** to enhance guest experience and meet sustainability targets.
*   Conduct regular audits to ensure compliance with **plant-based menu goals** and proper protein handling.
*   Streamline MTO processes for high-volume periods to maintain speed of service and quality.
