## Overall Station Structure
The Fresh & Fast (Grill) station features a **static core menu** including burgers, chicken sandwiches, hot dogs, and fries, complemented by a **Made-To-Order (MTO) Toppings Bar**. Enhancements include a **Daily Feature** and a **Vegan Option**. The playbook recommends a maximum of **1 Burger**, **2 rotating Daily Features**, a **Vegan Option**, and **French Fries**.

## Playbook Alignment
> **What's working**
> - The station structure aligns with the playbook's emphasis on **plant-based options** and **diversified proteins**, including a dedicated **Vegan Option**.
> - Adherence to **ingredient standards** for cage-free eggs, responsibly sourced seafood, and group-housed pork is expected.
> - **Chef tips** promote menu engineering strategies like swapping beef burgers for non-beef alternatives, supporting **plant-forward goals** and cost efficiency.
> - The playbook's commitment to **Coolfood Pledge** and **Net-Zero** targets is reflected in menu guidance.
> **Where to improve**
> - Monitoring progress towards the **44% plant-based goal** for core menu offerings is essential.
> - Strict adherence to **recommended maximum selections** is vital to prevent operational inefficiencies and maintain quality standards.
> - Ensuring proper **holding and service methods** for grilled proteins, as outlined in chef tips, is critical.
> - For large accounts, optimizing **Made-to-Order (MTO) execution** through simplified menus should be a focus.

## Rotation & Repetition Signals
* The station incorporates **rotation** through **2 additional Daily Features** to vary offerings.
* **Weekly protein diversification** is encouraged, with a recommendation to swap out at least one beef burger for a non-beef alternative.
* Menu engineering tips promote diversifying protein offerings with items like turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches to prevent repetition.

## Recommended Structural Adjustments
* **Strictly adhere** to recommended maximum selections to optimize efficiency and quality.
* **Prioritize protein diversification** and the integration of **plant-forward options** weekly.
* **Reinforce proper holding and service methods** for grilled proteins to maintain food quality.
* **Streamline MTO processes** for high-volume periods to ensure speed of service.
