The station structure is defined by **Core Offerings**, **Enhancements**, and **Recommended Maximum Selections**. For the Fresh & Fast (Grill) station, this includes a static core menu with Made-To-Order (MTO) toppings, supplemented by daily features and vegan options. Clear maximums are set to ensure efficient execution and quality.

## Playbook Alignment
> **What's working**
> *   **Ingredient standards** are well-defined, including **cage-free eggs**, **responsibly sourced seafood**, and **group-housed pork**.
> *   Strong commitment to **plant-based options** and the **44% plant-forward goal** by 2025.
> *   **Climate commitments** are clear, with targets for **net-zero GHG emissions** and a **25% reduction in US food-related emissions**.
> *   **Menu engineering tips** promote cost efficiency and customer satisfaction through protein diversification.
> *   Structured approach with **Core Offerings** and **Enhancements** provides a clear framework.

> **Where to improve**
> *   Ensure strict adherence to **proper holding procedures** for cooked proteins, avoiding water/broth baths.
> *   Monitor **Made-to-Order (MTO)** execution, especially in high-volume settings, to maintain speed of service.
> *   Prevent exceeding **Recommended Maximum Selections** to avoid operational inefficiencies and quality degradation.
> *   Verify that the **44% plant-based goal** is reflected in actual service menus, not just core templates.
> *   Regularly review protein diversification to ensure weekly non-beef alternatives are offered.

## Rotation & Repetition Signals
The playbook emphasizes rotation through **Daily Features** and the recommendation to "swap out at least one beef burger for a non-beef alternative each week." This promotes menu diversity and aligns with sustainability goals. Protein offerings should be diversified with options like turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches.

## Recommended Structural Adjustments
*   **Reinforce adherence** to **Recommended Maximum Selections** at all stations.
*   **Implement weekly rotation** of non-beef alternatives as per chef tips to meet **CPM reduction** and **plant-forward goals**.
*   **Audit MTO station execution** for efficiency, especially during peak times, and simplify menus if needed.
*   **Verify proper holding techniques** for all cooked proteins to maintain quality and safety.
*   **Track plant-based offerings** in actual service to ensure alignment with the **44% goal**.
