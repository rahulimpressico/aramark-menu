# Collegiate Hospitality: Residential Menu & Station Playbook

**Description:** This playbook is a quick guide to set the service expectations and guidelines for residential dining programs.

---

## Ingredient & Climate Commitments

### Ingredient Standards

* **Eggs:** We serve cage-free liquid and shell eggs to support animal welfare and responsible sourcing.
* **Seafood:** All seafood is responsibly sourced in accordance with Monterey Bay Aquarium Seafood Watch guidelines.
* **Pork:** Sourced from suppliers using group housing systems to reflect a commitment to improved animal welfare.
* **Plant-Based:** Plant-based meal options are available throughout the dining hall to accommodate diverse diets and promote sustainability.
* **Produce:** Fresh, seasonal fruits and vegetables are offered daily.
* **Diversity:** We feature regional and global menu items to celebrate culinary diversity and meet campus community tastes.

### Climate Commitments
* **Net-Zero:** The Science Based Targets Initiative (SBTi) has validated Aramark’s targets to reduce emissions and reach net-zero greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across the value chain by 2050.
* **Coolfood Pledge:** We are committed to reducing US food-related emissions by 25% by 2030.
* **Plant-Forward Goal:** By 2025, ensure that 44% of Collegiate Hospitality residential dining menu offerings are plant-based.
* *Note:* This means 44% of the entrees in the core menu template reflect this goal, not necessarily the actual service menu.

## Building Your Custom Residential Menu

### Core Offerings

* Identified to provide the minimum expectations of what should be offered at each station.
* Requirements vary by location, and Core stations may not fulfill all menu needs.

### Enhancements

* A set of recommendations added to Core Offerings to meet a location’s specific needs.
* Items should be added based on customer volume, contractual requirements, and client feedback.
* Items should not be added solely to fill space.
* Consider cost and labor capacity when enhancing offerings.

### Recommended Maximum Selections

* Provides a clear stopping point when adding items beyond Core and Enhanced.
* Exceeding these numbers may result in an inability to execute efficiently or meet quality standards.

## Station Guide: Fresh & Fast (Grill)

### Offerings Structure

* **Core Offerings:**
    * Static core menu with Made-To-Ingredients (burger, chicken sandwich, hot dog, fries, etc.).
    * MTO Toppings Bar.
    * Additional proteins can be added based on client requirements/demand.

* **Enhancements:**
    * Daily Feature (grilled chicken, hot dog, grilled cheese, etc.).
    * Vegan Option.
    * French Fries.

### Recommended Maximum Selections

    * 1 Burger.
    * 2 additional Daily Features (rotate).
    * Vegan Option.
    * French Fry.

### Chef Tips

* **Holding:** Do not hold cooked proteins in a water or broth bath.
* **Service Method:**
    * *Served Stations:* Grilled proteins should be cooked or finished to order.
    * *Self-Serve Stations:* Grilled proteins should be displayed in a single layer and shingled in a shallow pan.

* **Menu Engineering:**
    * Swap out at least one beef burger for a non-beef alternative each week; this lowers CPM by 18% without decreasing customer satisfaction.
    * Diversify protein offerings with turkey burgers, chicken, or fish sandwiches.
    * Incorporate MTO station take-overs, such as Grilled Cheese, instead of beef burgers.

* **Execution:** Made-to-order (MTO) may be difficult for large accounts; plan a simplified menu with a condiment/toppings bar to support speed of service.
